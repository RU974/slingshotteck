**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

Slingshotteck ("we", "us", "our") provides software products (HRMS and CRM), custom software development and IT services, including managed IT, networking, cybersecurity, cloud and IT consulting. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights you have.

By using our website at [www.slingshotteck.com](https://www.slingshotteck.com) or contacting us about our services, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

Slingshotteck is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can contact us at any time:

#### 2. Information We Collect

**Information you give us.** When you complete a contact, strategy-call, checklist download or application form, message us on WhatsApp, or email us, we may collect:

- Your name, email address and phone number
- Your business name, industry, website and location
- The content of your messages and any details you choose to share about your business needs
- Information you provide when applying to work with us

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit our website, we and our service providers may collect:

- Device and browser type, operating system and screen size
- IP address and approximate location
- Pages viewed, time spent, referring website and links clicked
- Cookie and similar technology identifiers (see Section 6)

**Information from other sources.** If you have engaged with our marketing, we may receive limited information from analytics and advertising platforms, or from publicly available business sources.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (such as health data or political opinions), and we ask that you do not send it to us.

#### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only where we have a lawful basis to do so:

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Responding to enquiries and booking strategy calls | Legitimate interests; steps prior to entering a contract |
| Providing and managing our services, invoicing and support | Performance of a contract |
| Sending requested resources, such as checklists | Consent or legitimate interests |
| Sending marketing emails or messages about our services | Consent, or legitimate interests where permitted by law |

We do not sell your personal information.

#### 4. Sharing Your Information

We share personal information only where necessary and with appropriate safeguards. Recipients may include:

- **Service providers** that help us operate, such as website hosting, email and form handling, CRM and scheduling tools, messaging platforms (including WhatsApp), analytics and payment processors. They may process data only on our instructions.
- **Professional advisers**, such as accountants, lawyers and insurers.
- **Authorities**, where we are required to do so by law or to protect our rights, safety or property.
- **A successor business**, if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case you will be informed of any change in how your data is used.

#### 5. International Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. Where your information is transferred abroad, we take steps to ensure it receives an equivalent level of protection, for example through adequacy decisions or approved standard contractual clauses.

#### 6. Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use:

- **Essential cookies**, which are needed for the site to function and cannot be switched off.
- **Analytics cookies**, which help us understand how visitors use the site. These are used only with your consent where required.
- **Marketing cookies**, which may be used to measure campaigns and show relevant advertising, only with your consent where required.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the website works. Third-party services we use, such as Google Tag Manager or WhatsApp, are subject to their own privacy policies.

#### 7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy:

- **Enquiries and leads:** up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client.
- **Client records and contracts:** for the duration of the relationship and up to 6 years afterwards for legal, tax and accounting purposes.
- **Marketing preferences:** until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.
- **Analytics data:** typically no longer than 26 months.

When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it securely.

#### 8. Protecting Your Information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and the use of reputable, security-conscious providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

#### 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete
- **Erase** your information in certain circumstances
- **Restrict** or **object to** our processing, including direct marketing
- **Data portability**, meaning receive your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing

To exercise any of these rights, email support@slingshotteck.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within one month (or as otherwise required by law). You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the United Kingdom this is the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

#### 10. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

#### 11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

#### 12. Changes to This Policy
