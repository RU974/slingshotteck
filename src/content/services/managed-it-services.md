## Why switch to managed IT?

Break-fix IT, where you call someone only when something goes wrong, is unpredictable and usually more expensive. Problems pile up, security updates are missed and downtime costs far more than prevention would have.

With **Slingshotteck managed IT services** you get a proactive team that looks after your technology every day for a predictable monthly cost. We already run critical networks, firewalls, Wi-Fi and IP telephony for busy offices, so we know what keeps a business online.

## What is covered

- **People:** helpdesk support, onboarding and offboarding of staff, account and password management.
- **Devices:** laptop and desktop setup, patching, antivirus/EDR and encryption.
- **Network:** firewall, switching, Wi-Fi and internet link monitoring, see [network infrastructure](/services/network-infrastructure).
- **Cloud:** Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and server administration, see [cloud solutions](/services/cloud-solutions).
- **Security:** MFA, email filtering, backups and regular reviews with our [cybersecurity team](/services/cybersecurity).

## How onboarding works

1. **Free IT assessment.** We document your users, devices, network, software and risks.
2. **Proposal and SLA.** A fixed monthly price with clear response and resolution targets.
3. **Stabilise.** We fix urgent issues, deploy monitoring and backups, and enable MFA.
4. **Run and improve.** Monthly reports, a technology roadmap and quarterly reviews.

## Who it is for

Managed IT works best for companies with roughly 5 to 250 staff that do not have, or do not want, a full in-house IT team: professional services firms, software houses, call centres, coworking spaces, clinics, retailers and logistics companies in the UK, USA and Pakistan.
