## Security that matches how attackers really work

Modern attacks rarely start with sophisticated malware. They start with a phishing email, a reused password, an unpatched VPN or a misconfigured cloud account. Our founder, Muhammad Usama Bin Ilyas, writes regularly about [threat hunting, social engineering and penetration testing](/blog), and that hands-on experience shapes every engagement.

## Who needs cybersecurity services?

- SaaS companies and software houses that must prove security to customers.
- Businesses handling payments or personal data under UK GDPR or US privacy laws.
- Companies preparing for ISO 27001, SOC 2 or Cyber Essentials and needing a gap review.
- Organisations that have had a breach or suspicious activity and need answers.

## How an engagement works

1. **Scoping call.** Agree targets, objectives, timing and rules of engagement.
2. **Testing.** Reconnaissance, scanning, manual exploitation and verification.
3. **Reporting.** Clear findings ranked by business risk, with fixes your team can apply.
4. **Remediation support.** Our engineers can implement fixes across your [network](/services/network-infrastructure), applications and cloud.
5. **Retest.** We confirm critical issues are closed.
