## Your network is the foundation of everything else

Cloud apps, VoIP phones, video calls, CCTV and payment systems all fail when the network fails. Yet many businesses run on a consumer router, a flat network and Wi-Fi access points installed wherever there was a socket.

Slingshotteck's engineers manage production networks every day, including multi-ISP firewalls, multi-floor Wi-Fi and IP telephony, so we design networks around real-world load, not a datasheet.

## Common problems we solve

- Internet drops when one ISP goes down.
- Wi-Fi that is fast in one room and unusable in the next, or disconnects during calls.
- Staff, guests and devices all sharing one flat network.
- Social media and streaming consuming bandwidth during working hours.
- No secure way for remote staff or branch offices to reach internal systems.
- Nobody knows the firewall password or how the network was configured.

## Our approach

1. **Survey and audit.** We document your current topology, devices, links and Wi-Fi coverage and identify risks.
2. **Design.** A written network design with VLANs, firewall rules, IP plan and Wi-Fi layout.
3. **Implement.** Out-of-hours cut-over to minimise disruption, with everything labelled and documented.
4. **Manage.** Optional monitoring, firmware updates and change management as part of our [managed IT services](/services/managed-it-services).

## Security first

Every network we build includes firewall hardening, admin access restricted by VPN and MFA, logging, and separation of sensitive systems. For a deeper review, pair it with a [vulnerability assessment or penetration test](/services/cybersecurity).
