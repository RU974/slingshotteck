## Move to the cloud with a plan

Moving to the cloud should make your business more resilient and flexible, not just more expensive. Slingshotteck starts with an assessment so every workload ends up in the right place: public cloud, private virtualisation or a mix of both.

## What we migrate and manage

- File servers and shares to SharePoint, OneDrive or Google Drive.
- Email to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.
- Line-of-business applications and databases to AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
- Web applications and SaaS platforms, including those we build through our [software development service](/services/custom-software-development).
- On-premises servers and firewalls virtualised on VMware ESXi.

## Our cloud migration process

1. **Assess:** inventory, dependencies, performance and cost baseline.
2. **Plan:** target architecture, security design, migration waves and rollback plan.
3. **Migrate:** test migrations first, then scheduled cut-overs out of hours.
4. **Optimise:** monitoring, backups, cost reviews and ongoing support through [managed IT services](/services/managed-it-services).
