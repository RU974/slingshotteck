## Technology decisions with confidence

Growing businesses often reach a point where IT has grown organically: a mix of tools, suppliers and quick fixes that nobody fully understands. Spending increases, risks hide in the gaps and new projects stall.

Slingshotteck's consultants have run IT for busy, multi-site operations, from firewalls and ISPs to HR, payroll and CRM systems, so our advice is practical and grounded in day-to-day operations.

## When to bring in an IT consultant

- You are growing quickly and IT is becoming a bottleneck.
- You are choosing an ERP, HRMS or CRM and want an independent view.
- You need to cut IT costs without increasing risk.
- You have had a security incident or failed an audit or customer security review.
- You are opening a new office or moving premises.

## How we work

1. **Discover:** interviews with leadership and staff, and a technical review.
2. **Assess:** findings on risk, cost and capability, benchmarked against good practice.
3. **Recommend:** a prioritised roadmap with budgets and quick wins.
4. **Deliver:** we can implement through our [managed IT](/services/managed-it-services), [cloud](/services/cloud-solutions) and [software development](/services/custom-software-development) teams, or support your chosen supplier.
