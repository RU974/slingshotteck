## A software development partner, not just a vendor

Off-the-shelf software forces your team to work around its limits. Custom software development lets you automate the processes that make your business different, connect the tools you already use and own the result outright.

Slingshotteck builds software for growing companies and startups in the **United Kingdom, United States, Pakistan and worldwide**. Because we also build and run our own products, [Slingshotteck HRMS](/hrms) and [Slingshotteck CRM](/crm), we understand what it takes to ship, host, secure and support software over years, not just weeks.

## What we build

- **Web applications and portals:** customer and supplier portals, internal admin tools, dashboards and reporting.
- **SaaS products:** multi-tenant platforms with subscriptions, onboarding, billing and analytics.
- **ERP systems:** finance, inventory, procurement, HR and payroll modules connected in one system.
- **Integrations:** APIs that connect payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Square), accounting software, biometric attendance devices and CRMs.
- **Modernisation:** rebuilding legacy desktop or spreadsheet-based systems as secure web applications.

## How a project runs

1. **Discovery (1 to 2 weeks).** We map your workflow, users and integrations, then agree scope, milestones and price.
2. **Design.** Wireframes and clickable prototypes so you can see the product before code is written.
3. **Agile build.** Two-week sprints, working demos and a shared board so you always know what is done.
4. **Testing and security.** Automated and manual QA, plus security checks informed by our [cybersecurity practice](/services/cybersecurity).
5. **Launch and support.** Deployment to the [cloud](/services/cloud-solutions) or your servers, training and an optional support retainer.

## Why companies choose an offshore team in Pakistan

Pakistan has a large, experienced pool of software engineers and a time zone that overlaps with both UK mornings and US afternoons. Working with Slingshotteck gives you a senior, English-speaking team at offshore rates, with one accountable company behind the work instead of individual freelancers.

## Security built in

Every application we deliver follows secure coding practice: input validation, parameterised queries, encrypted secrets, role-based access control and audit logging. For regulated data, we can align the build with UK GDPR and add penetration testing before launch.
