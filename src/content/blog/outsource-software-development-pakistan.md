UK and US companies increasingly outsource software development to Pakistan to access experienced engineers at offshore rates. Done well, it gives you a dependable, long-term team. Done badly, it leads to missed deadlines and code you cannot maintain.

Here is what we tell clients considering [offshore software development](/services/custom-software-development).

## Why Pakistan?

- **Talent:** a large, growing pool of developers skilled in PHP/Laravel, JavaScript, React, Node.js, mobile, cloud and DevOps.
- **English communication:** English is widely used in business and higher education.
- **Time zone overlap:** at UTC+5, Pakistani teams can overlap UK mornings, and evening shifts can cover US working hours.
- **Cost:** rates are typically far lower than equivalent UK or US in-house hires, without the overhead of recruitment and office space.

## Engagement models

| Model | Best for | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed-price project | Well-defined scope, MVPs | Agreed scope, milestones and price |
| Dedicated developer or team | Ongoing product development | Monthly fee, team works only on your product |
| Time and materials | Evolving requirements | Billed for actual hours, flexible scope |
| Support retainer | Live systems | Monthly hours for fixes and small features |

## How to vet an outsourcing partner

1. **Look at real work.** Ask for demos of products they have built and run, not just screenshots.
2. **Meet the people who will do the work,** not only sales staff.
3. **Check process:** sprints, demos, a shared task board, code reviews and automated testing.
4. **Ask about security:** how they handle credentials, access to your systems and secure coding.
5. **Start small:** a paid discovery phase or a two to four week pilot reveals more than any proposal.

## Protecting your IP and data

- Sign an NDA before sharing details.
- Your contract should assign all IP and source code to you on payment.
- Host code in a GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket organisation you own.
- Give the team the minimum production access they need, and revoke it when the engagement ends.

## Making it work day to day

Agree core overlap hours, use a single point of contact, hold a weekly demo and keep requirements in writing. Treat the offshore team as part of your company and they will act like it.
